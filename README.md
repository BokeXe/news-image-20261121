Saturday 21 November 2026 in one image: a newspaper front page with 54 date-checked
stories across 9 regions and 30 fields — three illustrated leads (Iran strikes Bahrain as tankers
hit in Hormuz; drones set the Russian cargo ship Sparta II ablaze off Finland; COP31
deadlocked over fossil fuels in overtime), a numbers strip, region coverage bars,
a UTC strip, and a full numbered story index. Every story is tagged by source and
confidence (confirmed / reported / claimed).

`main.py` copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, ≤1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`; both runs
write identical bytes, no network.
